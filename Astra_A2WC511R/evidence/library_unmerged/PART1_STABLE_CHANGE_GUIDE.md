# Part 1 Stable Reliability Tune Change Guide

Project: 2005 Subaru Outback XT Limited, USDM, 5EAT  
ROM ID: `A2WC511R`  
ECU ID: `2F12515306`  
Original ROM SHA256: `22D52A5B3DE7D86562EED2F85CCA772DB10E0949FCEDC2D61B7540B45F760218`

This guide is documentation only. It does not modify the ROM, does not create a candidate ROM, and does not authorize flashing.

## Part 1 Objective

Part 1 is a stable reliability tune for the installed VF52, GrimmSpeed 3-port EBCS, and 3-inch MAF housing. It is not a performance tune. It is intended to make the car safe for startup, idle, cruise, and careful partial-throttle logging while validating mechanical wastegate boost behavior.

The default Part 1 strategy is spring-pressure validation:

- Zero active base WGDC tables.
- Zero alternate max WGDC tables.
- Remove positive turbo dynamics authority.
- Preserve target boost, boost cut, knock control, DAM/IAM behavior, diagnostics, rev limiter, speed limiter, timing, MAF, fueling, and DBW tables.
- Do not require WOT pulls.

If near-zero logged WGDC still produces uncontrolled boost, the issue is mechanical or plumbing-related, not a table issue.

## Part 1 Exit Criteria

Do not proceed to a candidate ROM until the user explicitly approves this guide.

After any later approved Part 1 ROM is created and flashed, Part 1 passes only if logs show:

- WGDC stays near 0.0 percent during boost onset.
- Boost follows mechanical wastegate behavior without creep or overboost.
- DAM/IAM remains healthy.
- Feedback knock and fine learning knock do not show repeatable problems.
- Idle/cruise trims are reasonable enough to support later MAF review.
- No DTCs or limp behavior appear.
- No WOT pulls were required.

Part 2 performance tuning must wait for validated MAF/fueling/boost behavior and a loggable wideband for open-loop work.
